# Bar ledger — what changed in the bar, and how to trace it back

One entry per change to the bar (bardeck, waybar config, the scripts it runs):
the commit, what it did, what was verified and how, what was **not** verified,
and every side effect on the real machine. Newest round last. Times are local
(Europe/Zurich).

To undo one feature: `git revert <its commit>` (a merge: `git revert -m 1 <merge>`),
then `pkill -USR2 -x waybar`. To see one feature's code: `git show <commit>`.
